Engineering Principles

A simple standard for every Northframe release.

01 · QUALITY

Ship polished, not crowded.

Features should improve the product rather than simply increase its size. Performance, clarity, stability, and usability remain core release criteria.

02 · PRIVACY

Be clear about data practices.

Privacy information is maintained per application and updated when SDKs, data practices, or product functionality change.

03 · MONETIZATION

Respect the user's attention.

Advertising should fit natural product moments. Northframe avoids unnecessary interruptions and favors user-initiated rewarded experiences where appropriate.
