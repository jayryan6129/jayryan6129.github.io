## Evidence required before a seller is ranked
- Distinct store name and exact direct product URL from Shopee Philippines (not a search results URL).
- Same exact product brand, model, configuration, and variant as the catalog item.
- Product rating 4.5–5.0 AND seller rating 4.5–5.0; indexed ratings alone are not enough.
- Minimum 30 credible buyer reviews; prefer 100+; inspect recent negative feedback and suspicious patterns.
- Record review count, product/merchant ratings, verification timestamp, seller evidence and Mall badge as distinct fields.
- Rank only independently confirmed offers. Use 50% product rating, 25% seller rating, 20% credible review count (log scale), and 5% verified Shopee Mall status.
- Separate research links from Shopee affiliate-approved tracking URLs. Never activate commissions until account and offer eligibility are established.
- Recheck within 30 days, remove products that fail current eligibility or quality.

3. Confirm the same exact SKU across distinct stores, validate authenticity and Mall badge, and inspect adverse reviews.
4. Add the new entry to the catalog array only after obtaining evidence; leave unverified metrics unknown.
5. Display eligible researched candidates without a fabricated Top N rank. Once reviews and other quality evidence pass, assign actual quality ranks.
